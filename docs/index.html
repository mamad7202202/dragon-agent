modes

Three ways to talk to it

Switch per session — chat for thinking out loud, plan before touching files, agent when you mean it.

💬 chat

Pure conversation. No tools, no file access — fastest and cheapest.

🗺 plan

Read-only research (files, grep, web). Ends with a concrete numbered plan — nothing is modified.

🐉 agent

Full toolkit: read/write/edit/delete, shell, fetch. World-changing actions require your explicit approval.

why dragon

Memory is the product

Most agents forget you the moment a tab closes. Dragon remembers — precisely, and cheaply.

◈Session-scoped memory

Facts belong to the conversation they were learned in. Recall ranks them by relevance × importance × recency and injects only the top few — minimal tokens, maximal signal.

◇Global knowledge

Durable preferences travel across sessions via global facts and a plain MEMORY.md of standing instructions.

◆Permission gate

Writes, deletes and shell commands ask first. "Always allow" remembers your choice, and an independent explainer tells you what any action does before you decide.

▣Sessions everywhere

Every conversation is a resumable JSONL transcript. Continue last week's task in the GUI or the CLI — history, mode and memories come back with it.

▤BYOK presets

Google AI Studio, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, DeepSeek, Ollama, LM Studio — one-liner setup, keys never leave your machine.

◉Native speed

Pure Rust, hand-rolled SSE streaming, single static binary per platform. Millisecond cold starts, no Node, no Electron.
